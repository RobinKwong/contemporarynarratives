We are forming a community of interested artists, creatives, Research Software Engineers and a wide range of digital research technical professionals to exchange expertise, resources and opportunities.

We will deliver workshops in 2026 and 2027 bringing together creatives and data investigators (our way to refer to Research Software Engineers and the whole spectrum of digital research technical professionals, or dRTPs) to exchange their practice and make progress on data-driven creative projects.

If you would like to be part of this community and receive news about our events and resources first, send your expression of interest via this [**form**]({{ site.data.cde.join_form }}).

You can opt in to be invited to join the **Alan Turing Institute's AI&Arts group**, where we have a dedicated channel for announcements and to keep the conversation going. You will be part of an international community of over 600 arts practitioners, researchers, policymakers and funders, based in all continents, who are interested in working in the intersection of art and data. We also share information via email so you will also join our mailing list to receive news directly in your inbox. Community members also hear about our events and opportunities first.

If you are not ready to be part of the community yet and would simply like to receive updates via email, please sign up here: [Creative Data Exchange mailing list]({{ site.data.cde.mailing_list }}).

Your data is processed according to the Goldsmiths, University of London [privacy notice]({{ site.data.cde.privacy_notice }}).
